WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN JUST A FOOTBALL CLUB

Namanga River Football Club is built around a shared passion for football, teamwork and community.

We believe football is more than a game. It is an opportunity to bring people together, develop talent and inspire young people to pursue their ambitions.

Through discipline, commitment and unity, we continue working towards building a club that represents its community with pride.

OUR DIRECTION

OUR VISION & MISSION

OUR VISION

To become a respected football club recognised for developing talent, promoting teamwork and making a positive contribution to our community.

OUR MISSION

To promote football through discipline, teamwork, player development and community participation, while creating opportunities for our players to grow.
