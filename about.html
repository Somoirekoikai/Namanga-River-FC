About Namanga River FC

Building talent, inspiring communities and pursuing excellence through football.

WHO WE ARE

A Club Built on Passion

Namanga River Football Club is a football team driven by passion, discipline, teamwork and the desire to achieve excellence. We believe football is more than a game. It is a platform for developing talent, building friendships and bringing communities together.

Our ambition is to create opportunities for talented players, encourage young people to participate in sports and represent our community with pride.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a respected and competitive football club known for developing talent, promoting sportsmanship and inspiring the next generation.

Our Mission

To provide a positive football environment where players develop their skills, demonstrate discipline and work together to achieve success.

WHAT DEFINES US

Our Core Values

Teamwork

Working together and supporting one another on and off the pitch.

Discipline

Maintaining commitment, respect and responsibility.

Excellence

Always striving to improve and perform at our best.

Integrity

Playing fairly and respecting opponents, officials and supporters.
