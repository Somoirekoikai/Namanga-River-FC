WHO WE ARE

More Than a Football Club

Namanga River Football Club is a football club based in Namanga, Kenya. We are united by our passion for football, teamwork and community development.

Our goal is to provide an environment where talented players can develop their abilities, demonstrate discipline and compete with confidence.

Our Identity

Club: Namanga River Football Club

Location: Namanga, Kenya

Sport: Football

Our motto: One Club. One Dream. One River.

Our Vision

To become a respected football club known for developing talent, promoting teamwork and inspiring our community.

Our Mission

To develop football talent through discipline, hard work, teamwork and opportunities for competitive football.

Our Values

Discipline, respect, unity, commitment, sportsmanship and determination.
